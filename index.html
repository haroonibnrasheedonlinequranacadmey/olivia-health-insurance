Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions Before You Apply

View All FAQs
What information should I have ready for a quote?

ZIP Code, age, state, desired coverage type and contact information are a useful starting point.

Are Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum specific insurers?

No. They are common plan metal categories used to compare the relationship between premiums and costs when receiving covered care.

Can you help with Medicare or Medicaid?

The site provides general information and assistance pathways. Eligibility and final coverage depend on the applicable program and individual circumstances.

Can I request a consultation instead of a quote?

Yes. Use the consultation page to request WhatsApp, phone, office or online consultation.

Coverage Checklist

What to Review Before Choosing a Plan

Plan details differ by insurer, state, eligibility and enrollment period. Use these questions to organize your comparison.

Monthly Premium

Compare the amount you pay each month and how it fits your household budget.

Deductible & Out-of-Pocket Costs

Understand what you may pay before coverage starts and your potential annual cost limit.

Doctor & Hospital Network

Check whether your preferred doctors, hospitals and facilities participate in the network.

Prescription Benefits

Review covered medicines, tiers, copays and pharmacy-network requirements where applicable.

Preventive & Mental Health Care

Ask what preventive, behavioral and mental-health services are covered under the plan.

Family & Maternity Needs

If relevant, compare maternity, newborn, pediatric and dependent coverage details.
